# ADR-003: UUID Identifiers

## Decision
Use UUIDs for primary keys of JPA entities.

## Status
Accepted (implemented)

## Context
Entities require stable unique identifiers that are safe for distributed systems and external clients.

## Decision
Use UUID primary keys generated by JPA (GenerationType.UUID).

## Why We Chose It
- Avoids exposing incremental DB ids
- Better for distributed systems and merges

## Alternatives Considered
- Numeric auto-increment IDs (easy but reveal cardinality)
- ULID (alternative with sortable property)

## Advantages
- Universally unique
- No coordination required for ID generation

## Disadvantages / Trade-offs
- Longer keys (storage and indexing costs)
- Less index locality than sequential IDs

## Performance Implications
- May impact index fragmentation; consider UUID version strategy or UUIDv1/ULID if insertion locality becomes a problem.

## Interview Takeaway
UUIDs are safe and convenient for APIs and distributed systems; be mindful of indexing and storage implications.
